Your AI coding tokens, hatched into a companion — with your save in your own Google Drive.

A macOS menu-bar app — the original PokeTokenBar with the features I needed added.

What it does

  • Reads AI coding tool usage (Claude Code, Codex and others) from local log files on your Mac and turns it into a growing companion.
  • Google Drive save sync (optional): backs up your game progress to a PokeTokenBar_Save folder in your own Google Drive so you can continue on another Mac.
  • Based on PokeTokenBar with the features I needed added: Google Drive save sync, a separate app identity that installs alongside the original, and 30% cheaper eggs.

New: a livelier floating pet

  • Idle motions you switch on one by one: hop, walk around, look around, wiggle, bounce and sway. Frequency and walking range go from 1 (calm) to 5 (lively). The pet walks back toward where you placed it, never leaves the screen, and rests under macOS Reduce motion or Low Power Mode.
  • Click the pet when an agent finishes to jump to that agent's Terminal or iTerm2 tab (macOS asks once for Automation permission).

How save sync works

  1. Settings → Google sync → Sign in with Google, and approve in your browser.
  2. The first sync uploads your progress, or lets you choose between this Mac and a save already in Drive.
  3. On another Mac, sign in with the same account and pick the cloud save to continue.
  • What: the game save (companion, Pokédex, eggs, items) and difficulty. App settings stay on each Mac; usage logs are never uploaded.
  • Where: small JSON files in PokeTokenBar_Save in your own Drive. Files are named after the device that wrote them, and each device keeps only its newest 5 revisions.
  • When: at most every 30 minutes while the app runs, or on Sync now. Only changed progress is uploaded, so the battery and network cost is negligible.
  • Two Macs at once: nothing is overwritten. If both progressed, the app asks which save to keep and backs up the local one first.

How Google sign-in is used

The app requests only openid, email and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. drive.file lets the app see and manage only the files it creates; it cannot read any other file in your Drive. There is no developer server: the app talks to Google directly from your Mac. See the Privacy Policy.

한국어 요약

AI 코딩 도구 사용량(내 Mac의 로컬 로그)으로 포켓몬을 키우는 macOS 메뉴바 앱입니다. 선택 기능인 Google Drive 동기화는 게임 진행을 본인 Drive의 PokeTokenBar_Save 폴더에 백업하며, 앱이 만든 파일에만 접근하는 drive.file 권한만 사용합니다. 개발자 서버는 없습니다.

새 기능: 플로팅 펫이 깡충 뛰기·좌우로 돌아다니기·두리번거리기 등 자동으로 움직이고(동작별 켜기, 빈도·이동 범위 1~5단계), 에이전트 작업이 끝났을 때 펫을 클릭하면 그 Terminal/iTerm2 탭이 열립니다. 동기화는 앱 실행 중 최대 30분마다(또는 지금 동기화) 바뀐 진행만 올리며, 앱 설정과 사용량 로그는 올리지 않습니다. 두 Mac에서 동시에 진행하면 덮어쓰지 않고 어느 세이브로 이어갈지 묻습니다. 자세한 내용은 개인정보처리방침과 서비스 약관을 확인하세요.
