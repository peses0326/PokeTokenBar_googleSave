Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · applies to the PokeTokenBar_gs macOS app and this website

Summary

PokeTokenBar_gs runs entirely on your Mac. The app sends no data to the developer, who operates no server. If you turn on Google Drive sync, your save data goes from your Mac directly to your own Google Drive.

Data the app reads on your Mac

To grow the companion, the app reads token counts from local log files of AI coding tools (for example Claude Code and Codex). Only aggregate counts are used; prompt or code content is not stored or sent anywhere.

Google user data

Google sign-in is optional and only used for save sync. The app requests these scopes:

  • openid and email: to show which Google account is connected.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: to create and update save files in a PokeTokenBar_Save folder. This scope only grants access to files the app itself created. The app cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

How it is used. Game save files (progress, Pokédex, items) and optional usage summaries are uploaded to that folder and downloaded again on your other Macs. Nothing else is done with your Google data.

Where it is stored. Files live in your Google Drive. Sign-in tokens and your account email are stored only on your Mac, in the app's Application Support folder with owner-only file permissions. They are never sent to the developer or any third party.

Sharing. Google user data is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising, analytics, or training AI/ML models.

Limited Use. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your controls

  • Disconnect in the app's settings deletes the stored sign-in tokens from your Mac.
  • Revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete your cloud saves by deleting the PokeTokenBar_Save folder in Google Drive.
  • Local data can be removed by deleting ~/Library/Application Support/PokeTokenBar_gs/.

This website

This website (peses0326.github.io) uses Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to count visits. Google may set cookies and collect page views, referrer, browser and device type, and approximate location derived from your IP address. This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and is used only to understand how many people visit these pages. It is never linked to the app, to your Google sign-in, or to your Google Drive data. You can block it with your browser's cookie or tracking settings, or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
